# Security

## Reporting security issues

If you believe you found a security issue, do not open a public issue with exploit details. Use your organization's preferred private security reporting process.

## Public repository guidance

Before publishing changes:

- Do not include credentials, keys, tokens, connection strings, SAS URLs, private workspace URLs, or tenant-specific identifiers.
- Do not include customer data or operational vessel data unless it is explicitly approved for public release.
- Treat screenshots as data: review browser chrome, URLs, workspace names, user names, and notification content.
- Avoid committing generated exports that may contain hidden metadata.
- Follow your organization's open-source, privacy, trademark, and compliance review process before publishing under a company identity.
